About Dr. Pamerla Murali Dhar

Dr. Pamerla Murali Dhar is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Chemistry at GITAM School of Science, Visakhapatnam.

He has been associated with GITAM since 28 June 2008 and holds a Ph.D. in Chemistry from Andhra University, awarded in 2016. His doctoral research focused on synthetic transformations of bioactive natural products and the development of new synthetic methodology.

His academic work spans organic chemistry, organic synthesis, heterocyclic chemistry, catalysis and green chemical synthesis—areas that bring depth and context to the way Chemistry can be understood.
